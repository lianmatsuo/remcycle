# Code of conduct

remcycle is a small project with one maintainer. Everyone who takes part, in issues, discussions and pull requests, is expected to:

- Be kind, and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Stay on the subject. Insults, harassment, and personal or sexual remarks have no place here.
- Keep other people's private details private, including anything that came from their sessions or their memory files.

## When it goes wrong

Use **Report content** in the comment's menu to tell GitHub's moderators. The maintainer may hide or delete comments, lock threads, and block anyone who keeps breaking these rules.
